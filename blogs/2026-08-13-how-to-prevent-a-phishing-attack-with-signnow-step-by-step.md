Is SignNow a scam? No, SignNow is a legitimate eSignature platform. But confusion around the phrase “SignNow scam” arises when someone receives an unexpected signature request, when scammers imitate SignNow branding in phishing emails, or when a payment or cashback dispute involves a third-party service rather than SignNow directly.
