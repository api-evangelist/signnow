Quick answer: Sign the principal’s name first, then your own name, then a designation such as “Attorney-in-Fact” or “POA,” so the signature reads, for example, “Jane Doe, by John Smith, Attorney-in-Fact.” That single line is what separates a valid agent signature from one that a bank, title company, or government office can reject on sight....
